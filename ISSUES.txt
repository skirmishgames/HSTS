HEROES SYSTEM TACTICAL SCALE: ISSUES TO BE ADDRESSED
Last updated: 2026-11-09 (site version hts-v28)

HOW TO USE THIS FILE
- One entry per issue. Never delete an entry. Change its status and add a dated line under "Decision".
- Status: OPEN, DECIDED (answer chosen, not yet applied), FIXED (applied and shipped), WONT FIX.
- When two sources disagree, record both under "Conflict" and the winner under "Decision".

CONFLICT RESOLUTION ORDER (proposed, change it if you disagree)
1. Your physical tile, card or counter in hand.
2. The newest official rulebook or booklet (Rulebook V4.1, Scenario Booklet V2.5).
3. Older official files (Scenario Booklet V2.4), the 2023 errata, then the tile and option art.
4. Community files, Wikipedia and my own reading of images.
A decision that overrides the site's current data must say which source won and why.
