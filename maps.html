Game box

Pieces

Piece type

Squares wide and high

Drag a piece to move it. Click a piece to select it, then use the buttons or the keys: arrows move, R rotates, F flips, D duplicates, Delete removes, Ctrl+Z undoes.

Terrain pictures come from the Heroes of Normandie files for each box. One square is one square of the general grid, and a board is 7 by 7 squares. Pieces are cut to whole squares, so tiles line up on a board when you snap to whole squares.
