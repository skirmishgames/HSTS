# Things to check against your own copies

This list holds everything on the site that was read from scans, guessed, or decided without the physical game in hand. If you own the box or expansion, please compare and report what is wrong: games@staroversky.com. Say which item, what the site shows, and what your copy shows.

Each item says where to look on the site and what to compare.

## Army builder rules

1. **Stripe matching.** An option fits a slot when it has the same shape (long or narrow) and every stripe colour on the option is also on the slot. Check any tile you know well: does the option picker offer exactly the options that fit in real life?
2. **Tiles that take two stripe patterns.** A few V2 tiles record two accepted patterns as one set of colours (the US Rangers tile is blue, yellow and red). An option with only some of those colours fits. Are there options offered on these tiles that should not be?
3. **Beavertails (Battle for Caen), German army.** The booklet's army puts a Magnetic Mine (black and white stripes) in a black and yellow SS-Panzergrenadiere slot. The builder shows a warning for it. Is the booklet right, or is it a printing slip?
4. **Bluff.** The German Bluff option costs 5 in Heroes of Normandie and 10 in Heroes of Stalingrad. Both are offered under Germany. Are both costs right?
5. **Mixed German armies.** Germany holds tiles from Heroes of Normandie and Heroes of Stalingrad together. The order token line counts Stalingrad tiles only. Is there an official rule for mixing the two games?
6. **Support tile.** It takes any V2 option, whatever its shape or stripes (Battle for Caen booklet V1.4, p.11). First edition options are kept off it. Is that right?

## Heroes of Stalingrad

7. **Heroic Platoons.** Die Geisterabteilung and Sasha and the Beasts have three narrow slots, and their own options are narrow. Check against the punchboard.
8. **Duel of Snipers.** The Zaytsev and Major König slots are treated as long, and their options (Heroic, Op' Fire, Grenade) as long.
9. **Costs.** Every Stalingrad tile and option cost was read from scans. Please check them against your cards.
10. **Tokens and markers.** The event, END and Objective markers are described from how they look. The Stalingrad scenario books were not checked for their exact wording.

## First edition (V1) armies

11. **Slot stripes.** On first edition tiles each slot shows the platoon's colours, a black band, or half of each. A platoon-colour slot takes that platoon's options, a black slot takes black-stripe options, a half-and-half slot takes either. The stripes for each slot were read from the scans by colour. Check them in the tables at the end of this page.
12. **Categories.** Options are filed by the colour of the name on the card: yellow is an order, red is gear, white is support. Traits such as Veteran, Tricky, Ferocious and Trigger Happy are therefore filed as gear.
13. **Army size limits.** High Command tiles show "500+" and Field Command tiles "200+". The builder shows this as a note and does not enforce it. What is the exact rule?
14. **Hero tiles.** First edition hero tiles are entered with no slots. Do any of them have a slot?
15. **Editions kept apart.** First edition options only fit first edition tiles, and V2 options do not fit first edition tiles. A single army can still hold tiles of both editions. Should that be allowed?
16. **Unit lists.** The units shown under each tile and option were read from the small pictures, and their kinds (officer, infantry, vehicle) were guessed from the names.

## Pictures

17. **Punchboard sides.** Local scan names say Front and Back. They come from the "a" and "b" in the source file names and were not checked against the printed sides.
18. **Bloody Omaha.** These pictures still use older, smaller cuts because no good scan of the front is held: the Panzergruppe and Panzer Aufklärungs tiles and their options (Kübelwagen, Recon Group, Stummel Group, Panzerschreck, StuG IV, Panther, German Tank Gear and Tank Ammo), the four aircraft tiles, the minefields, M10, M3A1 Halftrack, Bazooka, Bangalore, Explosive M1, Rangers Gear, Mobile Fire, Urban Fighter, Carbine M1 and Guardian. Better scans are welcome.
19. **Promos.** The KS-9 and SOLO promo tiles and options, and the Knife option, have no better scan. The FFI Colonel Bastien sheet held is a small, heavily compressed proof and is not on the site.
20. **Icons.** The icon pictures come from the rulebook PDFs at small size. Sharper versions are welcome.

## Scenarios and My collection

21. **What each scenario needs.** 57 scenarios are tagged with the boxes they need. Colombelles Castle and Juno Beach are assumed to draw their Commonwealth units from Epsom, Charnwood & Goodwood. The rest say "Needs not tagged" and are never hidden. Corrections and missing tags are welcome.
22. **Fan scenarios.** Only fan scenarios that name their map tiles are kept. If one is missing and does name its tiles, say so.

## Archive

23. **Editions of documents.** Unofficial Expansions, Unofficial Scenarios and the Realism Variant are filed as first edition. Full Throttle (light vehicles, 2020) is filed under Big Red One. The unit bibles are all filed as first edition.
24. **First edition rules.** The first edition core rules were removed because the Big Red One rules override them. Four expansion rules stay (Mines, Commonwealth army, British Commandos, FFI recruitment). Say if any of these is also replaced by a V2 rule.
